CLEANING THE WASHER DRUM

Use Affresh washer cleaner monthly. Run the Clean Washer cycle with an empty
drum. Do not use bleach as the primary drum cleaner on this revision.

Wipe the door seal with a damp cloth after each load to reduce odor.
