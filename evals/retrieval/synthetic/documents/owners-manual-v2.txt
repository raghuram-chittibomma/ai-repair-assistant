CLEANING THE WASHER DRUM

Use Affresh washer cleaner monthly. Run the Clean Washer with affresh cycle
with an empty drum. This revision adds: leave the door ajar between loads so
the drum can air-dry, and clean the drain-pump filter every three months.

Do not mix chlorine bleach with Affresh tablets.
